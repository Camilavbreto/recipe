Pancakes

  • Time: 30 mn
  • Serves: 8-10
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 2
Sugar 1/2 cup
Milk 1 cup
Vanilla extract A splash
Oil 1 tablespoon
Salt A pinch
Flour 2 cup

Preparation:

  1. First take the egg,sugar and milk in a bowl.
  2. Then, mix them and then put the vainilla extrac, oil, salt and mix again
  3. Finally, add the flour little by little and mix quickly until you have a smooth batter that is neither too runny nor too thick.
  4. Turn on the heat and warm the pan with a little cooking spray or butter. Cook until you see bubbles, then flip it over .

Chocolate chip cookies

  • Time: 25-30 mn
  • Serves: 12-16
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 1 cup
Sugar 1/2 cup
Melted butter 1/2 cup
Egg 1
Vainilla extract 1 teaspoon
Salt 1 pinch
Baking powder 1/2 teaspoon

Preparation:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. In a bowl, mix the melted butter and sugar.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract and mix well.
  4. Add the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  5. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  6. Form small balls of dough and place them on a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes until golden.

Brownie

  • Time: 35-40mn
  • Serves: 8-12
Ingredients Quantity
All-purpose flour 1 cup
Sugar 1 cup
Cocoa powder 1/2 cup
Egg 2
Vainilla extract 1 teaspoon
Oil 1/2 cup

Preparation:

  1. Prepare your oven: Preheat to 180 °C (350 °F) so it’s ready when the batter is mixed.
  2. Mix wet ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, sugar, oil, and vanilla until smooth and slightly pale.
  3. Add cocoa: Sift in the cocoa powder to avoid lumps, then stir until fully combined and the mixture looks glossy.
  4. Incorporate flour: Gradually add the flour, mixing gently with a spatula or whisk until just combined. Do not overmix—this keeps the brownies tender.
  5. Prepare the pan: Lightly grease or line a square baking pan (about 20×20 cm / 8×8 in) with parchment paper.
  6. Bake: Pour the batter into the pan, spread evenly, and bake for 25 minutes. The top should look shiny and slightly cracked.
  7. Cool and cut: Allow the brownies to cool in the pan for at least 10 minutes before slicing into squares.
